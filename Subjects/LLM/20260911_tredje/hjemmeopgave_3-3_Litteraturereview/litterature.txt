Keep — low overlap with hallucination and ambiguity
These treat drift as distribution / representation / prompt-surface change, not as “the model is wrong about the world” and not as “the prompt has several readings.”


PaperWhy it stays distinct


When Meaning Stays the Same, but Models Drift (PBSS)

Meaning is held fixed; only tokens change. That is the opposite of an ambiguity study. Behavior shift ≠ fabricating facts.

Natural Context Drift Undermines NLU

Answer is still in the passage. Failure is mismatch with pretraining surface form, not underspecification and not invention of facts.

Semantic Anchors in In-Context Learning

Can ICL override pretrained label semantics? About rigidity of meaning under few-shot flips, not vague prompts or made-up content.

Feature Drift: How Fine-Tuning Repurposes Representations

SAE / internal features change activation under SFT. Training mechanics, not output truth or prompt underspec.

Value Drifts: Tracing Value Alignment During Post-Training

Stance/value shift through SFT vs DPO. Alignment dynamics, not factual errors or ambiguous wording.

Measuring and Controlling Persona Drift

Specified persona decays over turns (attention decay). Consistency of a constraint, not truthfulness or ambiguity.

Sem-DPOP

reference-tuned prompt generators wander from the user’s intended wording. Optimization fidelity; only light contact with “meaning change.”

AI RESPONS:

Here is a curated set of free, reputable papers that treat semantic drift (or a closely related drift of meaning/representations) in LLM training and inference/prompting. All are on arXiv and/or ACL Anthology.

Core inference / generation papers
1. Know When To Stop: A Study of Semantic Drift in Text Generation

Spataru, Hambro, Voita, Cancedda — NAACL 2024 (Meta FAIR)

arXiv PDF · ACL Anthology
Defines semantic drift as generated text leaving the prompt’s subject, with falling relevance/truthfulness. Shows LLaMA-2 style models emit correct facts first, then incorrect ones. Introduces a semantic drift score on FActScore biographies and studies early stopping / reranking. Best single starting paper for generation-time drift.



2. When Meaning Stays the Same, but Models Drift: Evaluating Quality of Service under Token-Level Behavioral Instability in LLMs

Li, Kreuzwieser, Peters — arXiv 2025 (ICML Tokshop track)

arXiv
Prompting-focused. Same semantic intent, different tokenizations/wordings → systematic response shifts (prompt variance). Introduces Prompt-Based Semantic Shift (PBSS). Directly about inference instability under meaning-preserving rephrasing.




3. Natural Context Drift Undermines the Natural Language Understanding of Large Language Models

arXiv:2509.01093

HTML
Inference-time QA: human-edited passages that still contain the answer but have drifted away from pretraining text. Accuracy collapses as similarity to training versions falls (e.g. large drops on BoolQ). Bridges training distribution vs. inference context.




4. Semantic Anchors in In-Context Learning: Why Small LLMs Cannot Flip Their Labels

Kumar — arXiv:2511.21038

arXiv
Few-shot prompting cannot override pretraining label semantics (1–12B models). ICL refines a semantic backbone; inverted demos do not produce coherent anti-semantic classifiers. Strong paper on what prompting can and cannot change.




5. Measuring and Controlling Persona Drift in Language Model Dialogs

arXiv:2402.10962

HTML
Multi-turn inference: persona specified in the prompt decays within ~8 self-chat turns. Links drift to attention decay; proposes split-softmax. Useful if your “prompting” setting is long dialogues.

Training / post-training / representations





6. Feature Drift: How Fine-Tuning Repurposes Representations in LLMs

Galichin et al. — EACL 2026 Findings

ACL Anthology
SAE analysis: instruction tuning mostly reuses base features with changed activation distributions (“feature drift”), rather than inventing new concepts. Case study on refusal. Training-time counterpart of semantic/representational drift.





7. Value Drifts: Tracing Value Alignment During LLM Post-Training

arXiv:2510.26707

HTML
Tracks how expressed values shift through SFT vs preference optimization on Llama-3 / Qwen-3. SFT usually locks values; later DPO-style stages rarely realign them. Training-time value drift, not lexical drift.





8. Sem-DPO: Mitigating Semantic Inconsistency in Preference Optimization for Prompt Engineering

arXiv:2507.20133

HTML
Preference-tuned prompt generators can win reward while drifting from the user’s meaning. Formal bound on semantic drift; semantic-consistency weights in DPO. Training of prompt policies, not the base LLM itself.

Related but still on-topic (multimodal / decoding / older NLP)





9. Curing Semantic Drift: A Dynamic Approach to Grounding Generation in Large Vision-Language Models

Chen et al. — arXiv:2506.21509

HTML
Decoding-time drift: linguistic priors detach from the image. Training-free Dynamic Logits Calibration. Same “drift during generation” idea as (1), for LVLMs.





10. The Telephone Game: Evaluating Semantic Drift in Unified Models

Mollah et al. — arXiv:2509.04438

arXiv
Iterated T2I ↔ I2T like the telephone game; measures compounding loss of entities/attributes. Protocol + metrics (MCD, MGG). Inference composition, not standard text-only prompting.




11. Addressing Semantic Drift in Question Generation for Semi-Supervised QA

Zhang & Bansal — 2019

PDF
Classic seq2seq QG paper: generated questions drift from context/answer. Semantics-enhanced RL rewards. Useful as the pre-LLM lineage of the term.

overview paper / introduction:

Characterizing and Measuring Linguistic Dataset Drift
Chang, Lee, Connor, Misra, Toutanova — ACL 2023
ACL Anthology

It treats drift as a property of data and models, not as “the model starts lying.”
It splits drift into vocabulary / structural / semantic dimensions. That is a taxonomy you can keep using.
Semantic drift there means meaning change not reducible to word-frequency change — close to your project, far from hallucination and far from ambiguity.
It is about predicting performance under shift, which lets you say: LLMs inherit this problem at training and at inference.


Use it to open: NLP already distinguishes kinds of linguistic drift; LLMs make those kinds show up in prompting, pretraining match, ICL, and fine-tuning. Then PBSS, Natural Context Drift, Semantic Anchors, Feature Drift, and Value/Persona Drift become instances, not competitors with this paper.

(ICL and SAE are two entirely different concepts that relate to diagnosing, tracking, and understanding why and how this semantic drift occurs:)
