Distributed systems foundation:


IDempotence: idempotence means that we want to ensure that if i do multiple operations (clicking a payment)
 the system will only perform the actions once (den laver kun 1 payment).

immutability: the database is not allowed to destroy data.
(destroy == update eller delete)
en maade er at have flere versioner af den samme data.


Heterogenous network:
hver microservice skal kunne aendres / scaleres separat.
